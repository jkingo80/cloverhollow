A cozy farm game with no ads

No banner ads, no "watch a video to continue", no pop-ups. Clover Hollow is a calm farming game for iPhone where the only thing on screen is your farm.

Get it free on the App Store

Free to download · Optional in-app purchases

How it's free without ads

The game is free to download and play. If you want to speed things up you can buy clovers, the in-game currency, or the one-time Starter Pack. You never have to, and you're never shown an ad to push you there. Clovers you earn or buy never expire.

Parents: purchases go through Apple, so Ask to Buy and Screen Time work as usual.

What makes it cozy

  • No rush. Play for two minutes or two hours. The farm moves at your pace.
  • Gentle goals. Neighbour orders, market stalls and an album of stamps to fill, page by page.
  • Lots to do. Crops, orchards, seven kinds of animals, and a kitchen full of goods to bake and craft.
  • Plays offline. No internet needed, anywhere.
  • Private. No account and no tracking. Your farm lives on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

Who it's for

Players who want a few calm minutes with a coffee, people who loved classic farm games but got tired of ads and energy bars, and parents looking for something gentle and ad-free for kids.
