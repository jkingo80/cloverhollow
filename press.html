Short description

Clover Hollow is a cozy offline farming game for iPhone. Plant crops, raise animals, bake and craft, and run a little farm market, at your own pace and with no ads.

About the game

Your aunt left you her little farm at the edge of the woods. In Clover Hollow you plant fields of wheat, pumpkins, strawberries and sunflowers, raise chickens, cows, sheep, goats, ducks, pigs and alpacas, and turn what you grow into bread, pizza, cheese, juice, salsa and quilts.

Fill neighbour orders, load the delivery truck, stock your market stalls and earn a stamp in your album for every good you make. There's no timer chasing you and no ads interrupting you, and the whole game works without an internet connection, so it's as happy on a plane as it is on the sofa.

Clover Hollow is made by one developer. It collects no personal data: no account, no tracking, and saves live on the device and in the player's own iCloud.

Features

  • Grow crops and orchards, tap to harvest
  • Raise seven kinds of farm animals
  • Bake, cook and craft lots of goods
  • Neighbour orders, a delivery truck and market stalls
  • Album stamps for every good you make
  • Plays fully offline, iCloud save
  • No ads, no account, no tracking
