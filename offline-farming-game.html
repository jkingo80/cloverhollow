What works offline

All of it. Clover Hollow doesn't use a game server, so the full game runs on your iPhone:

  • Crops and orchards. Wheat, pumpkins, strawberries, sunflowers and fruit trees.
  • Animals. Chickens, cows, sheep, goats, ducks, pigs and alpacas. Collect eggs, milk and wool.
  • Baking and crafting. Bread, pizza, cheese, juice, salsa, quilts and more.
  • Orders and the market. Fill neighbour orders, load the truck and stock your stalls.
  • Album stamps. Earn a stamp for every good you make.

Why offline matters

Many farm games need a connection to load, save or show ads. That's a problem on a plane, in the subway, abroad without roaming, or for kids on a tablet without data. Clover Hollow is built the other way around: everything happens on the device, and your own iCloud keeps a copy for when you get a new iPhone.

Because nothing goes to a server, there's also nothing to track. No account, no ads, no third-party analytics. See the privacy policy.

Common questions

Does it work in airplane mode?

Yes. Download it before you fly and play the whole trip.

Do crops keep growing while I'm offline?

Yes. Timers run on your iPhone, so crops and goods finish while the game is closed.

Will I lose my farm?

No. It saves on your iPhone and syncs to your iCloud next time you're online.

Can I buy things offline?

Purchases go through Apple, so those need a connection. Everything else doesn't, and buying is always optional.
